The 4 Winning Rules

Official Guidelines for the 3-Hour Campus Paw-Safari

📸 Rule 1: Strict 3-Sided Photos

Every animal must have Front View, Left Profile, and Right Profile photos. Incomplete angles won't qualify for points!

🐕 Rule 2: Maximum Animals Covered

The volunteer who documents the highest number of unique qualified campus dogs & cats wins.

🏷️ Rule 3: Every Resident Must Be Named

Give every animal a known name or creative nickname (use the 🎲 Dice button if unknown!). Blank names disqualify the spot.

⚡ Rule 4: Tie-Breaker: Earliest Time Wins

In case of a tie in total animals spotted, the volunteer who recorded their winning batch earlier in the morning takes the trophy!
